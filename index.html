Questions, answered

What is BTST trading?

Buy Today, Sell Tomorrow. You buy a stock near market close and sell it the next trading day, capturing the overnight gap-up or morning momentum. The scanner is built specifically for this style.

What exactly is the "fuel remaining" filter?

The model estimates a stock's total expected move, then subtracts what it already moved today. A stock up 2% with an 8% expected move has 6% fuel left — a good BTST candidate. A stock already up 8% has almost no fuel, and gets filtered out no matter how good its chart looks.

Does the model really learn?

Yes. Every pick is tracked to its real outcome the next morning. The ensemble re-weights its scoring genes based on results — genes that pick winners gain influence, genes that pick losers lose it. Monthly stats (win rate, average return) are shown in every daily message so you can verify it yourself.

How much capital do I need?

The scanner works for any size. Many BTST traders start with ₹20,000-50,000 across 1-2 positions. Always use the suggested stop-loss and never risk more than you can afford to lose.
